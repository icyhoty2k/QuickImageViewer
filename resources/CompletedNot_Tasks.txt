One Potential Bottleneck: The std::function Overhead
You are pushing std::function<void()> tasks into your queues.
The Cost: Every time you std::move a std::function or capture variables in a lambda, you might trigger a heap allocation if the lambda closure is larger than a small pointer size (usually 16–32 bytes).
The Fix: If you find the decoder thread is "stuttering" during very high-speed scrolling, check the size of the lambdas you are pushing. For the g_decoderWorker, try to capture pointers rather than objects to keep the closure size under the "Small Object Optimization" limit, which avoids heap allocations during the task dispatch.
